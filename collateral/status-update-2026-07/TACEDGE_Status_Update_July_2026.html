TACEDGE is a field platform for ground-engineering delivery — one connected workflow from project setup to a smarter next project.

What TACEDGE Is

A project is configured once. Operators capture drilling, installation, grouting, testing and incidents where the work happens. The project manager reviews evidence and exceptions and releases a trusted report — and every confirmed record improves the setup of the next project.

Value Proposition
  • One work item. One connected record.

    Setup, field activity, QA and release stay connected from the workface to closeout.

  • Assurance, not admin.

    Project managers assure the work—not assemble the report. Engineers and clients receive confirmed, evidence-backed reports.

  • Compounding learning.

    Confirmed records improve future project layouts, checks and recommendations.

Where We Are
  • Working prototype live across desktop and field mobile — configure, capture, QA and release end to end.
  • Used on recent ground-engineering delivery in New Zealand — see the site video.
  • Built first for anchoring and drilling — adaptable to adjacent ground-engineering workflows.
  • Scoping first validations with civil contractors — one narrow workflow at a time, on real work.
  • Born on the 2019 Whakaari / White Island recovery operation. Mission: to bring shared clarity to the tactical edge.
